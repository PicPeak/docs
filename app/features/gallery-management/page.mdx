# Gallery Management

Galleries in PicPeak are organized as **events**. Each event has its own slug, password, expiration date, and set of photos organized into categories.

## Creating events

From the admin dashboard, create a new event by providing:

- **Event type** --- wedding, corporate, birthday, portrait, or custom.
- **Event name** --- displayed as the gallery title.
- **Event date** --- the date of the event.
- **Customer name and email** --- used for email notifications.
- **Password** --- optional; when set, guests must enter it to view the gallery. The Information card can also **hide the logo on the password page** for a single event.
- **Expiration** --- number of days until the gallery expires (default: 30 days).

Each event receives a unique slug and a shareable gallery link.

## Publishing

When you publish, the dialog offers **Send the gallery email now**, ticked by default. Unticking it publishes quietly: the gallery goes live and nothing is sent — no gallery email, no customer notice, no WhatsApp message. Use it when you want the link live before the client hears about it, or when you will tell them yourself.

Afterwards, **Send gallery email** on the event page sends the link whenever you are ready. You confirm the password first if the gallery has one. Re-sending to a gallery you already notified is allowed; drafts, expired and inactive events are not.

## Previewing as a guest

**View Gallery** on the event page opens the gallery the way guests see it, skipping the password even on protected galleries and working for drafts. It relies on your admin session, so the URL is useless to anyone who is not signed in as an admin.

The preview follows the admin [session timeout](/guides/admin-settings/security#session): once your admin session has been idle for too long, the gallery shows *Your admin session has expired. Sign in again to preview this gallery.* with a link to the login page. Browsing the preview counts as activity. (Builds containing [PicPeak PR #1497](https://github.com/PicPeak/picpeak/pull/1497).)

The preview does not create a guest session and stays out of your statistics: it is not counted in access logs, download counts, per-photo views, or the notification bell.

## QR codes and printed cards

The event's Share link card carries a QR code for the gallery, with a live preview and four downloads: **PNG**, **SVG**, **Table card (A6)** and **Poster (A4)**.

The PDFs contain the event name, the QR code, a caption ("Scan to view & share your photos") and the share URL, and follow your admin interface language — they are available in English, German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian and Slovenian. No logo or branding is applied to them.

## Categories

Photos within an event can be organized into categories (e.g., "Ceremony", "Reception", "Portraits"). Categories help guests navigate large galleries.

A category can act as a **filter**, leaving its photos in the main grid, or as a **folder**, which takes them out of the grid and puts them behind a tile the guest opens. Categories also carry a cover photo, a per-category download permission, and their own order. See [Photo Categories & Folders](/features/photo-categories).

## Uploads

PicPeak supports drag-and-drop upload directly in the admin panel. Files can also be uploaded via the API.

- Supported image formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP
- Maximum file size: 50 MB per image
- Batch uploads respect the `general_max_files_per_upload` setting (default: 500)
- Thumbnails are generated automatically during upload

## Expiration and archiving

When an event reaches its expiration date:

- The gallery becomes inaccessible to guests.
- PicPeak automatically archives the event and its photos to the `events/archived` directory.

Expiration warnings can be sent via email before the gallery expires.

## Guest uploads

Events can optionally allow guests to upload their own photos. This is useful for collaborative events where multiple people contribute photos.

Guest uploads respect the event's **Photo cap**: once it is reached, further uploads are refused and the uploader shows a message naming the limit. A guest upload is only filed under the event's own categories or global ones. (Builds containing [PicPeak PR #1495](https://github.com/PicPeak/picpeak/pull/1495).)

## Bulk downloads

Guests can download all photos from a gallery (or a filtered subset) as a ZIP archive with a single click.

## Download limit

A gallery can cap how many photos the client downloads, for packages such as "10 photos included". Set **Download Limit** on the Create Event or Edit Event form; empty or `0` means unlimited, which is the default. New events take their value from [Event Defaults](/guides/admin-settings/event-defaults).

**What counts.** The limit counts distinct photos. A photo uses a slot the first time it is downloaded in any way: single download, *Download selected*, *Download all*, or a download at another resolution. Downloading the same photo again is free, so an interrupted or repeated download does not cost anything. Videos count like photos.

**What the client sees.**

- A counter such as **3 of 10 downloads used** above the gallery.
- When selecting photos, what the selection costs. Photos already downloaded cost nothing. A selection that needs more than is left is flagged before the download starts, and its download button is disabled.
- *Download all* is disabled while the gallery holds more photos than the remaining downloads.
- Once the limit is used up, download buttons are disabled for photos not yet downloaded, and a refused download shows *Download limit reached. Please contact your photographer for more downloads.*

A download that does not fit is refused as a whole: a ZIP is never delivered with some photos silently left out.

**Full-size originals.** While a limit is set, the lightbox shows guests the preview version (at most 1920 px) of photos they have not downloaded, not the original. Otherwise the original would be one "Save image as" away and the limit would not mean anything. Videos have no preview version, so they stay playable in full.

**Who is counted.** The limit applies to the gallery as a whole, not per guest. Customer-portal sessions count against it. Your own admin preview never does.

**Usage and reset.** The event page shows the usage, for example **7 / 10 downloaded**, next to the download protection badges. To give the client more downloads, raise the limit in the edit form. **Reset** clears the usage: the client gets the full limit back, and photos they already downloaded count again. Lowering the limit below what is already used is allowed; the gallery then shows no downloads left, and nothing already downloaded is taken back. Duplicating an event copies the limit but not the usage.

The v1 API accepts `download_limit` on `POST /api/v1/events`.

Available on the beta channel in builds containing PicPeak/picpeak PR 1568.

## Filtering and search

The gallery interface supports:

- Filtering by likes, ratings, comments, or favorites
- Searching by filename
- Sorting by date, name, size, or rating
- Exporting filtered selections as ZIP or generating Capture One/Lightroom-compatible file lists
