# Customer Accounts

Recurring clients --- studios, agencies, repeat private customers --- often book multiple shoots over time. Customer accounts give them a single login that lists every gallery they have access to, replacing the per-gallery URL + password juggling.

This is a separate user tier from your admins:

| Tier | Logs in at | Sees |
|---|---|---|
| Admin | `/admin/login` | Everything they have permission for |
| Customer | `/customer/login` | A dashboard listing every gallery they're assigned to, no per-event passwords |
| Guest | `/gallery/<slug>` (per-event password) | One specific gallery |

The per-event-password and shareable-link flows continue to work unchanged --- customer accounts are an additional path, not a replacement.

## Enabling the customer portal

Customer accounts are gated behind a feature flag:

1. Open **Settings > Features**.
2. Enable the **Customer Accounts** card in the CRM section.
3. The **CRM** entry appears in the admin sidebar; **Accounts** is one of its sub-pages.

<Callout type="info">
The `customerPortal` flag controls **admin UI visibility** (sidebar entry, /admin/clients pages), not access revocation. Existing customers keep working when the flag is off --- their `/customer/*` endpoints still respond, their gallery sessions stay valid. To revoke access for a specific customer, use one of the per-record tools below instead.
</Callout>

## Inviting a customer

From **CRM > Accounts**:

1. Click **Invite customer**.
2. Enter their email; optionally pre-fill name, company, address, and other profile fields.
3. PicPeak emails an invitation link with a single-use 7-day token.
4. The customer follows the link, creates a password, lands on `/customer/login`.

After accepting, the customer profile is editable on both sides --- the admin from the customer detail page, the customer from `/customer/profile`.

Pending invitations show on the **Invitations** tab and can be cancelled from there. Once an invitation email has gone out, it cannot be resent from the Messages archive — send a new invitation instead.

## Assigning galleries to a customer

Two ways to wire a customer to a gallery:

### From the event editor

The **Customer accounts** picker on the Edit Event form is a multi-select autocomplete --- search by email or company. Anyone you add gets the gallery on their dashboard immediately.

### From the customer detail page

The **Manage galleries** dialog on the Assigned events card lets you reconcile a customer's full assignment list in one round-trip. Useful when a single customer has many galleries to add or remove at once.

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Add an event chip | Customer's dashboard shows it on next refresh |
| Remove an event chip | Customer's gallery JWT for that event is **invalidated on the very next request** (returns 403 with `CUSTOMER_ASSIGNMENT_REVOKED`) --- they're booted out instantly, no token blacklist needed |

Removing an assignment doesn't affect the per-event-password flow --- guests with the password can still open the gallery if it has one. Only the customer's own login-based access is revoked.

## Documents

Customers can exchange files with you in the portal: you share documents with them, they upload signed contracts and other files for you to review, and you can request specific documents with automatic reminders. Beta channel only. See **[Customer Documents](/features/customer-accounts/documents)**.

## Customer dashboard

After login, the customer sees a dashboard listing every assigned gallery with thumbnails, dates, and status. Clicking through opens the gallery directly --- no per-event password required, even if the gallery has one configured for guests.

The dashboard header (logo + company name) is configurable from **Settings > Branding > Customer dashboard header**. Toggle each off if you want a more minimal customer-facing shell.

## Locking out a customer

Three different actions, three different scopes:

| Action | Mechanism | Scope | Real-time? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Remove from one gallery | "Manage galleries" dialog removes the junction row; `verifyGalleryAccess` re-checks on every customer-minted JWT | One gallery | Yes (next request) |
| Deactivate the account | Sets `customer_accounts.is_active = false` and bumps `password_changed_at`; rejects every outstanding JWT | All galleries | Yes (next request) |
| GDPR-erase the account | Wipes profile + invalidates everything. Signed contracts are kept; see [What erasing a customer keeps](/features/crm/contracts#what-erasing-a-customer-keeps) | All galleries; profile data anonymised | Permanent |
| Force re-login | Send password-reset email; on apply, bumps `password_changed_at` | All galleries | On apply |

All four are on the customer detail page under **Account actions**.

**Erasing a customer also cleans up their mail** (stable from v3.133.4, beta from v3.136.0-beta.0, [PicPeak PR #1598](https://github.com/PicPeak/picpeak/pull/1598)). Every email still queued for the customer's address — a quote reminder, a document request reminder, a newsletter — is cancelled and never goes out. On every queued, sent, failed or cancelled email to that address, the recipient and the template variables are replaced with a redaction marker; the row itself, its email type and its timestamps stay as the audit trail. The address is matched case-insensitively. On beta, earlier addresses the customer held are matched too, for the time they held them, and contract mail sent to a signer's own address is cleaned up for the unsigned contracts the erasure redacts.

**Logging out of the portal** also ends the gallery sessions the customer opened from it (builds containing [PicPeak PR #1497](https://github.com/PicPeak/picpeak/pull/1497)). Gallery sessions opened from the portal before that update are not linked to the portal login; they expire on their own within 24 hours.

## Sub-features (in progress)

The **CRM** sidebar section is shaped to host more than just Accounts --- the architecture supports per-customer Calendar, Quotes, Bills, and Messaging surfaces that ship as separate features. When a sub-feature is enabled in **Settings > Features**, its sub-nav entry appears automatically inside `/admin/clients`. Sub-features that are still off don't show.

### Per-customer CRM toggles

When the CRM features (`quotes`, `bills`, `contracts`) are enabled globally, each customer carries individual override columns:

| Column | Effect when off |
|---|---|
| `feature_quotes` | Customer's Quotes tab hidden; quote PDFs refused |
| `feature_bills` | Customer's Bills tab hidden; invoice PDFs refused |
| `feature_contracts` | Customer's Contracts tab hidden; contract PDFs refused (migration 131) |
| `feature_hours` | Customer's Hours tab hidden (rarely useful customer-facing) |

Gating is **master AND per-customer** as of PR #622 — an admin disabling Bills globally is honoured for customers even if their per-customer `feature_bills=true`. Earlier the customer routes checked only the per-customer column; this is now closed via the shared `customerFeatureAllowed()` helper that wraps `getEffectiveFeaturesForCustomer()`.

Defaults for newly-created customers are configurable in **Settings > CRM > Customer surface defaults**.

## Email notifications

Customer-related emails follow the same template system as the rest of PicPeak (**Settings > Email > Templates**, under the **Customers** category):

- `customer_invitation` --- the invite-link email
- `customer_password_reset` --- admin- or self-triggered reset
- `customer_gallery_assigned` --- digest sent when an admin adds new gallery access (one email per save, not one per gallery). It is not sent for a draft gallery; publishing the gallery announces it.

All three carry hand-translated `en` + `de`; other locales are machine-translated and clearly flagged in the Templates editor. The document emails are listed on [Customer Documents](/features/customer-accounts/documents#email-templates).

### Gallery emails reach assigned accounts

<Callout type="info">
Beta channel only: from v3.165.0-beta.0 ([PicPeak PR #1819](https://github.com/PicPeak/picpeak/pull/1819)), with the "complete gallery" mail from v3.166.0-beta.0 ([PR #1837](https://github.com/PicPeak/picpeak/pull/1837)) and the portal links from v3.168.3-beta.0 ([PR #1916](https://github.com/PicPeak/picpeak/pull/1916)). On stable the gallery email goes to the event's customer email, and to the assigned accounts only when that field is empty.
</Callout>

A gallery has two kinds of recipient: the customer email on the event, and the customer accounts assigned to it. Every gallery announcement — creating a live gallery, publishing a draft, **Send gallery email**, resending it, and "your complete gallery is ready" — reaches both:

- The event's customer email gets the normal gallery email.
- Every assigned account that can act on it gets `customer_gallery_assigned` (the complete-gallery mail in its own version), linking into its portal at `/customer/events/<slug>`, in the account's preferred language. An account can act on it when the customer portal is on, the account is active and it can sign in; a passive account (created in the CRM, never invited) is not mailed.
- When the customer email belongs to one of the assigned accounts, that person gets one mail: the normal gallery email when it carries a welcome message or client access (link and PIN), otherwise the portal version.
- Accounts are mailed only when the admin sending holds `customers.events`. Without it the accounts stay assigned but are not told, and the dialog says how many are not notified.
- Accounts are not told about a draft, archived or expired gallery.

**A gallery shared only through the portal needs no password.** When you create a gallery with assigned accounts, no customer email and no password, and at least one account can be mailed, PicPeak locks the share link with a generated password that is never shown, stored recoverably or mailed. Adding a customer email to such a gallery later asks you to set a password (`GALLERY_PASSWORD_REQUIRED`). A reachable account also satisfies **Require customer email** under [Event Defaults](/guides/admin-settings/event-defaults).

**A CRM customer always gets their portal link.** Whenever a message with a gallery link goes to an address that belongs to an account assigned to that gallery (and the account can sign in), the link points into the portal, not at the direct gallery link: the gallery email, the gallery password reset mail, the expiry warning, the renamed-gallery mail, the complete-gallery mail and the WhatsApp message on create and publish. This holds whoever sends the message, including the hourly expiry job. A passive account keeps the direct link and the gallery password.

### No administrative emails

<Callout type="info">
Beta channel only, from v3.173.0-beta.0 ([PicPeak PR #1989](https://github.com/PicPeak/picpeak/pull/1989)).
</Callout>

Some customers want the amount, not your mail. The **No administrative emails** switch on the customer's edit form (shown when Quotes, Contracts, Invoices or Customer documents are on) holds back every quote, contract, invoice, reminder, Storno and customer document email addressed to that customer. The documents are still issued as usual: numbered, stored, in the tax report and, with portal access, in the customer's portal. A badge on the customer header shows that the switch is on.

- **Held back, not dropped.** Each held-back email is logged with the status **Suppressed** in the email log. Copies to other recipients of the same document, such as an accountant in CC, are held back with it. Emails already queued when you turn the switch on still go out.
- **Only this customer's own addresses.** The switch applies when the mail goes to the customer's login or billing email. A co-signer at another address, your own notifications and another customer who shares a billing inbox are not affected.
- **Not affected:** account mail (invitation, password reset), the contract signing code, gallery emails and PicTransfer emails.
- **Send becomes Issue** on that customer's quotes, contracts and invoices, and **Resend** is hidden on an issued invoice.
- **Reminders** become **Record reminder**: the reminder level and overdue status are recorded, but no late fee is added, since a fee nobody was told about cannot be charged.
- **Contracts:** the customer's own signature is opened in the portal instead of sent by link, and the signing certificate says so. Resending the link, reminders, and asking the customer for missing details by email are refused (`409 NO_ADMIN_EMAILS`); enter the missing address on the customer instead. Without portal access the contract is signed on paper and uploaded.

## Permissions (RBAC)

Three new admin permissions ship with the customer portal:

- `customers.view` --- see the Accounts page
- `customers.create` --- invite, edit assignments, deactivate, reset passwords
- `customers.delete` --- GDPR-erase

Granted to `super_admin` and `admin` roles by default. Editor and viewer roles are intentionally locked out --- managing customer records is a write-class operation on a different user tier.
